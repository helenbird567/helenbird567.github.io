# Helen
### Founder and Director
### Swoop Inventories

## Our History...
My first experience of the value of a professional inventory service came as a landlord having to one day ask the tenant for money for the repairs to a damaged property. 

After wasting too much of my precious time I learnt the importance of having a comprehensive inventory report to minimise any potential dispute.  Finding a professional service, I could trust, to deliver concise, high quality assessments quickly to my inbox proved hard. 

This is my motivation in setting up Swoop Inventories to provide inventories that are a fair and accurate reflection of the property. We firmly believe in offering a great customer service and professional comprehensive reports by using the latest cloud based software.
